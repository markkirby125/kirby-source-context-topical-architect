## When to Use
- You are onboarding a new or existing site and must lock **Entity Identity (`Max 2 Things`)** and explicit **Wanted vs. Unwanted Attribute Boundaries** before writing a single page (Module 1).
- You are replacing bloated keyword dumps (`379k`-keyword exports) with a **Core-First commercial service architecture** (e.g., 68 distinct service pages before any informational blog posts) (Module 1).
- You are architecting a topical map by scraping **top-8 competitors' sitemaps** to map `QDP/QDH/QDS` (`Question-Document-Paragraph / Heading / Section`) for **`>20%` broader commercial/intent coverage** using `65–70%` semi-automated briefs plus human structural editing (Module 2).
- You are wiring informational answer/attribute pages to commercial money pages via **1-link natural in-body contextual buyer-journey bridges** instead of `20`-link footer/nav blocks (Module 3).

## Mandatory Governance & Epistemic Guardrails
- **Unobservable Leak Guardrail:** References to `siteFocusScore` and `siteRadius` come from leaked-attribute / practitioner commentary (`RankOS`); live Google numeric scores are **not observable**. Never fabricate numeric focus/radius scores (`100/100`) — enforce structural pass/fail gates only.
- **Directional / Single-Source Tagging:** Benchmarks cited from Kasra Dash (`86/172 = 50.0%` Nov 10 Core Update cohort, `"do braces hurt"` SERP audit, `RankOS` 5-step flow, `68`-service dental sitemap) and Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR (`>20%` coverage across 8 sitemaps, `65–70%` automated briefs, `QDP/QDH/QDS`) are directional practitioner methods, not Google SLAs.
- **Core-Update Freeze:** Never prune or delete existing off-axis URLs during an active Google core/spam update or its 7-day settle window (`../../kirby-seo-telemetry/SKILL.md` Module 16 §16.2).
