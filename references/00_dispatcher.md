## When to Use
- You are onboarding a new or existing site and must lock **Entity Identity (`Max 2 Things` — entity-level commercial specialty families, not individual service-page counts)** and explicit **Wanted vs. Unwanted Attribute Boundaries** before writing a single page (Module 1).
- You are replacing bloated keyword dumps (`379k`-keyword exports) with a **Core-First commercial service architecture** (e.g., `68` distinct dental service pages organized inside `1–2` core specialty families before any informational blog posts) (Module 1).
- You are architecting a topical map by scraping **up to 8 eligible direct commercial competitors' sitemaps** (or all available eligible direct commercial competitors when fewer than 8 exist) to map `QDP/QDH/QDS` (`Query Deserves a Page / Heading / Section`, Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR, `Af9rvlVPQCQ`, Nov 11, 2026) using `65–70%` semi-automated briefs plus human structural editing (house workflow convention) (Module 2).
- You are wiring informational answer/attribute pages to commercial money pages via **1-link natural in-body contextual buyer-journey bridges** instead of redundant `20`-link location blocks (Module 3).

## Mandatory Governance & Epistemic Guardrails
- **Unobservable Leak Guardrail:** References to `siteFocusScore` and `siteRadius` come from leaked-attribute / practitioner commentary (`RankOS`); live Google numeric scores are **not observable**. Never fabricate numeric focus/radius scores (`100/100`) — enforce structural pass/fail gates only.
- **Directional / House-Protocol Tagging:** Benchmarks cited from Kasra Dash (`86/172 = 50.0%` Nov 10 Core Update cohort, `"do braces hurt"` SERP audit, `68`-service dental/maxillofacial sitemap) and Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR (`QDP/QDH/QDS` framework, `"Google Ranking Secret"`, `Af9rvlVPQCQ`, Nov 11, 2026) are directional single-source practitioner methods, not Google SLAs; the `65–70%` semi-automated brief split is a kirby house workflow convention.
- **Core & Spam Update Freeze:** Never prune or delete existing off-axis URLs during an active Google core update or its 7-day settle window, or during a spam update before rollout completion + the 7-day settle window clears (`../../kirby-seo-deployment/SKILL.md` Module 7 §7.12 gate (d) and `../../kirby-seo-telemetry/SKILL.md` Module 16 §16.2).
