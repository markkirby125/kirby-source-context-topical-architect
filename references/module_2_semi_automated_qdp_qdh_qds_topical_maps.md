## 2.1 The 8-Competitor Sitemap Scraping & `>20%` Coverage Protocol
Instead of inventing topical maps from zero-context LLM brainstorms or unfiltered keyword exports:
1. **Scrape Top 8 Commercial Competitors' Sitemaps:** Extract existing commercial service, comparison, attribute, and supporting URLs across the top **8** niche competitors (filtering strictly through Module 1's List A/List B Wanted vs. Unwanted Attribute Boundaries — reject competitor bloat that fails the 4-Question Page-Focus Audit).
2. **Map Every Validated Query to Structural Slot (`QDP / QDH / QDS`):**
   - **`QDP` (Query Deserves a Paragraph):** Supporting nuance or sub-attribute answered as a concise `40–100`-word standalone block inside a parent page.
   - **`QDH` (Query Deserves a Heading):** Distinct buyer sub-question (`H2`/`H3`) that requires its own dedicated heading and answer capsule inside an existing hub or service page (`also applies Query Deserves a Heading` SOP).
   - **`QDS` (Query Deserves a Section / Standalone Page):** High-intent commercial variation or independent attribute node passing the SERP-overlap test (`../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 2 §2.37) that warrants a dedicated URL or major structural section.
3. **The `>20%` Coverage Target:** Build the clean unified map to cover **`>20%` more relevant, on-axis commercial & buyer-attribute questions** than any single competitor (`more relevant depth on Wanted Attributes`, never off-axis trivia).

## 2.2 The 65–70% Semi-Automated Brief & Human Structural Gate
1. **Hybrid Content Brief Generation (`65–70%` Automated / `30–35%` Human):**
   - Use structured sitemaps, GSC queries, `PAA` chains, and buyer-segment matrices to semi-automate **`65–70%`** of the page brief (`target entity`, `QDP/QDH/QDS` headings, attribute checklist, internal bridge targets).
   - Require **human editorial judgment for the final `30–35%`**: pruning off-axis headings, verifying factual claims against first-party evidence, locking `What's Included / What Isn't` scopes, and enforcing voice/modality parity.
2. **Mandatory Pre-Publish Drift Check:** Before drafting any new URL on the map, pass the candidate list through `../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 20 (`publish-gate.md`) to ensure zero overlap with existing URLs and strict entity proximity (`<= 2` logical hops).
3. **Cadence & Subfolder Maturation Handoff:** For large directories or multi-hundred-page builds, never dump the entire map on Day 1. Route rollout pacing to `../../kirby-seo-deployment/SKILL.md` Module 7 §7.2 (3-month subfolder maturation followed by randomized daily publishing batches).
