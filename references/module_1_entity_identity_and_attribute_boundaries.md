## 1.1 The End of Volume-Driven "Topical Authority"
1. **The Nov 10, 2026 Core-Update Cohort (Mid-Rollout Audit Conducted Nov 14, 2026; Single-Source):** A preliminary mid-rollout audit conducted Nov 14 across `172` sites cohorting around the Nov 10, 2026 Google Core Update reported **`86 of 172` (`50.0%`)** losing visibility; the auditor attributed the losses primarily to off-topic informational drift away from what the business actually sells (conversion effect unverified).
2. **SERP Evidence (`"do braces hurt"` / `"do fillings hurt"` — Nov 13, 2026):**
   - `"do braces hurt"` (`1,100` searches/mo, `KD 26`, `658` clicks): AI Overview + 1 dental site + PAA + Reddit + Discussions/Forums + Video carousel appear before the second organic URL at Position 7; **9 of the 10 websites ranking in 2024 dropped out of the top 10 by Nov 2026**.
   - `"do fillings hurt"` shows an AI Overview at #1 followed by Colgate (`DR 84`). Practitioner claim (Kasra Dash): chasing ~500 informational blog posts around head terms is ineffective for most commercial SMBs.
3. **Unobservable Leak Boundary:** Commentary around `siteFocusScore` and `siteRadius` reflects leaked-attribute analysis (and proprietary tools like `RankOS`). Live Google scores cannot be queried; treat focus and radius as **strict structural gates**, never invented numerical scores.

## 1.2 Week-1 Entity Identity & Wanted vs. Unwanted Attribute Boundaries
Before drafting a single page or running keyword research, complete this **1-week alignment deliverable** with the business owner:

1. **The Max-2-Things Anchor:** State what the entity **is** and which **1–2 primary commercial specialty families** (entity-level capabilities before mapping every real sub-service under §1.3) it wants Google and AI answer engines to associate with it (e.g., *"Emergency & General Plumbing in Austin"* — NOT *"plumbing, HVAC, kitchen remodeling, and home tips"*).
2. **Two Mandatory Attribute Boundary Lists:**
   - **List A — Wanted Attributes:** Specific commercial services, buyer segments (`accountants` vs. `landlords` vs. `students`), materials, compliance credentials, and buyer problems the business genuinely delivers and wants to own in SERPs and LLM citations.
   - **List B — Unwanted Attributes (Explicit Negative Boundaries):** Adjacent, high-volume, or low-margin topics the site must **refuse** even when keyword tools show traffic.
     - *Example (Unrelated Legal & Price-Diluting Hosting Offers):* Under §1.2 item 3's keyword-export drift trap below, reject `"cheapest way to get a divorce"` (unrelated legal intent) and reject `"cheapest shared hosting"` when selling managed enterprise hosting (`"cheap"` attaches an unwanted price attribute).
     - *Example (Dentistry):* Reject `"dentist salary"`, `"how to cure gum disease without a dentist"`, and `"dog dentist"` (`2,400` searches/mo on `"dog dentist"`, unless veterinary dentistry is an explicit business offering).
     - *Example (Plumbing/Heating):* Never mix off-axis posts (`"What is SEO?"`, `"Best cake recipe?"`) into a core service footprint (e.g., `~23` core service hubs cited via `kirby-aiseo-skill` Module 20 §20.5).
3. **Keyword-Export Drift Trap:** Raw keyword tool dumps (`"dentist"` → `379,000` keywords; UK `"braces"` → `133,000` keywords pulling `"trousers"`, `"knee"`, `"belt"`) corrupt topical maps. **Never build topical maps from unfiltered head-term exports or blind competitor sitemap clones.**

## 1.3 Core-First Commercial Architecture (Services Before Blogs)
1. **Commercial Intent Beats Informational Volume:**
   - `"teeth whitening"` (`61,000` searches/mo, triggers Google Map Pack + direct bookings) vs. `"does teeth whitening hurt"` (`200` searches/mo, AI Overview + 4 one-page blogs averaging `15` visits/mo and zero bookings).
   - A real commercial service page (`Bupa` teeth-whitening service page: `>7,500` visits/mo — single-source practitioner figure; derived illustration vs. zero-booking blogs) outperforms a ranking informational blog (`15` visits/mo) by roughly `500×` (hundreds of times).
2. **The 68-Service-Page Multi-Specialty Commercial Blueprint:** Before writing any informational blog content, map and build dedicated commercial pages only for **real sub-services actually delivered by credentialed providers** within Week-1's `Max 2 Things` ceiling (illustrative single-source example spanning two clinical specialty families — general/cosmetic **dentistry** [implants, Invisalign, composite bonding, children's sedation] and **oral/maxillofacial surgery** [jaw surgery, oral biopsy, therapeutic jaw Botox] — totaling **68 distinct commercial service pages**, never creating thin doorway pages without distinct service delivery).
3. **4-Question Initial Page-Focus Screen (Run First on Every Existing & Planned URL; surviving candidates then pass [`kirby-aiseo-skill`](../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md) Module 20 (`module_20_pre_publication_semantic_drift_gate.md`, which emits `publish-gate.md`) final pre-publication gate):**
   1. *Does this page help someone about to buy what we actually sell?*
   2. *Does it strengthen an attribute from List A (Wanted Attributes) without triggering List B?*
   3. *Can it realistically win an organic click on today's AI Overview SERP? (For commercial money pages only; informational answer/attribute bridge pages mark Q3 `N/A` and are evaluated under Q4's bridge condition.)*
   4. *Does it move the visitor one concrete step closer to buying? (Informational answer/attribute pages pass Q4 only when carrying one natural Module 3 contextual bridge within the 1-link cap, targeting a List-A money page or the next-hop page of a Module 3 chain that terminates at one.)*
   - **Action:** **New/planned URLs:** any single failure = rework/reject. **Legacy assets:** two or more failures only **nominate candidates** for consolidation and pruning; every nominated eviction must still clear ALL `§7.12` safety gates before execution (`../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 2 §2.34 & `../../kirby-seo-deployment/SKILL.md` Module 7 §7.12 — backlinked URLs must be `1:1 301` never `410`, `90-day` zero/near-zero GSC impressions check [not clicks alone] with `<90d`/seasonal exemptions, four safety gates, and no execution during an active core/spam rollout or its `7-day` post-rollout settle window).
