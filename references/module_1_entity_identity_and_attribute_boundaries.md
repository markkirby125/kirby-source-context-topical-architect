## 1.1 The End of Volume-Driven "Topical Authority"
1. **The Nov 10, 2026 Empirical Collapse Pattern (Single-Source):** Across `172` audited sites hit by Google's Nov 10, 2026 Core Update, **`86 of 172` (`50.0%`)** suffered from the identical failure mode: informational blog content drifted away from what the business actually sells, leaving zero-click AI Overview queries with near-zero conversions.
2. **SERP Evidence (`"do braces hurt"` / `"do fillings hurt"` — Nov 13, 2026):**
   - `"do braces hurt"` (`1,100` searches/mo, `KD 26`, only `658` clicks): AI Overview + 1 dental site + PAA + Reddit + Discussions/Forums + Video carousel appear before the second organic URL at Position 7; **9 of the 10 websites ranking in 2024 disappeared by Nov 2026**.
   - `"do fillings hurt"` shows an AI Overview at #1 followed by Colgate (`DR 84`). Chasing 500 informational blog posts around head terms is obsolete for commercial SMBs.
3. **Unobservable Leak Boundary:** Commentary around `siteFocusScore` and `siteRadius` reflects leaked-attribute analysis (and proprietary tools like `RankOS`). Live Google scores cannot be queried; treat focus and radius as **strict structural gates**, never invented numerical scores.

## 1.2 Week-1 Entity Identity & Wanted vs. Unwanted Attribute Boundaries
Before drafting a single page or running keyword research, complete this **1-week alignment deliverable** with the business owner:

1. **The Max-2-Things Anchor:** State what the entity **is** and which **1–2 primary commercial capabilities/attributes** it wants Google and AI answer engines to associate with it (e.g., *"Emergency & General Plumbing in Austin"* — NOT *"plumbing, HVAC, kitchen remodeling, and home tips"*).
2. **Two Mandatory Attribute Boundary Lists:**
   - **List A — Wanted Attributes:** Specific commercial services, buyer segments (`accountants` vs. `landlords` vs. `students`), materials, compliance credentials, and buyer problems the business genuinely delivers and wants to own in SERPs and LLM citations.
   - **List B — Unwanted Attributes (Explicit Negative Boundaries):** Adjacent, high-volume, or low-margin topics the site must **refuse** even when keyword tools show traffic.
     - *Example (WordPress Hosting):* Explicitly reject `"cheapest way to get a divorce"` because it attaches the brand to `"cheap"` and unrelated legal intent.
     - *Example (Dentistry):* Reject `"dentist salary"`, `"how to cure gum disease without a dentist"`, and `"dog dentist"` (`2,400` searches/mo).
     - *Example (Plumbing/Heating):* Never mix off-axis posts (`"What is SEO?"`, `"Best cake recipe?"`) into a 23-page core service footprint.
3. **Keyword-Export Drift Trap:** Raw keyword tool dumps (`"dentist"` → `379,000` keywords; UK `"braces"` → `133,000` keywords pulling `"trousers"`, `"knee"`, `"belt"`) corrupt topical maps. **Never build topical maps from unfiltered head-term exports or blind competitor sitemap clones.**

## 1.3 Core-First Commercial Architecture (Services Before Blogs)
1. **Commercial Intent Beats Informational Volume:**
   - `"teeth whitening"` (`61,000` searches/mo, triggers Google Map Pack + direct bookings) vs. `"does teeth whitening hurt"` (`200` searches/mo, AI Overview + 4 one-page blogs averaging `15` visits/mo and zero bookings).
   - A real commercial service page (`Bupa` teeth-whitening service page: `>7,500` visits/mo) outperforms a ranking informational blog (`15` visits/mo) by two orders of magnitude.
2. **The 68-Service-Page Commercial Blueprint:** Before writing any informational blog content, map and build dedicated commercial pages for **every real sub-service** the business actually performs (e.g., a dental practice sitemap spanning **68 service pages**: jaw surgery, oral biopsy, jaw Botox, children's sedation, implants, Invisalign, composite bonding).
3. **4-Question Page-Focus Audit (Run on Every Existing & Planned URL):**
   1. *Does this page help someone about to buy what we actually sell?*
   2. *Does it strengthen an attribute from List A (Wanted Attributes) without triggering List B?*
   3. *Can it realistically win an organic click on today's AI Overview SERP?*
   4. *Does it move the visitor one concrete step closer to buying?*
   - **Action:** Pages failing all four checks are consolidated into stronger commercial/attribute pages or scheduled for post-freeze pruning via `../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 2 §2.34 and `../../kirby-seo-deployment/SKILL.md` Module 7 §7.12.
