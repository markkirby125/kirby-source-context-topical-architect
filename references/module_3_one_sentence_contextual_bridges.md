## 3.1 The 1-Link Natural Contextual Buyer-Journey Bridge
1. **Replace Redundant Repeated Link Lists:** Replace redundant `20`-link repeated city/service body-or-footer SEO link lists (`"location blocks"`) with focused contextual pathways while keeping the primary sitewide `<nav>` and global `<footer>` crawl paths intact PLUS preserving `../../kirby-local-seo/SKILL.md` Module 7 Rule 7's `2–4` genuine adjacent-suburb links and Module 11's Core 30 internal linking circuit (`../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 2 §2.34 Hub & Crawl-Path Protection checkpoint); preserve those required local links and replace only the redundant repeated link block.
2. **The Logical-Conclusion Bridge Rule:**
   - At the logical conclusion of each **single-answer or BOFU attribute page**, place **one natural, sentence-level in-body contextual link** (strictly capped at `1` contextual in-body bridge per single-answer/BOFU attribute page, aligning with [`kirby-aiseo-skill`](../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md) Module 2's Contextual In-Body Bridge Link Discipline `0–1` BOFU budget (Module 10 §10.11F); broader TOFU hubs follow Module 10 §10.11F's `10–30` in-body link budget) leading to the *next logical buyer question* (sourced from `PAA`, Ahrefs question exports, or `GSC` query data) until the user reaches the commercial service page (`Money Page`).
   - *Chain Example:* `[Answer/Attribute Page: "Why composite bonding chips on front teeth"]` → 1 contextual in-body bridge → `[Comparison/Pricing Page: "Composite bonding vs porcelain veneers cost"]` → 1 contextual in-body bridge → `[Core Service Page: "Composite Bonding in [City] — Book Consultation"]`.
3. **Near-Zero Distracting Internal Links on Money Pages:** Once the user arrives on the commercial service/booking page, enforce near-zero distracting internal nav/cross-links inside the body (`../../kirby-text-first-cro/SKILL.md` §6.4 and `../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 10 §10.11F) so visitor attention flows exclusively to the primary CTA (`Call` / `Book Online`).

## 3.2 Buyer-Segment Attribution & Commercial Progression Telemetry
1. **Map Buyer Differences Explicitly:** Avoid generic feature pages (`"accounting software"`). Segment bridge pathways by concrete buyer roles (`accountants` vs. `landlords` vs. `students`) documenting each segment's unique **problems, budget ceilings, compliance constraints, and urgency triggers**.
2. **Track Commercial Progression, Not Vanity Blog Traffic:**
   - Measure commercial-progression attribution per `../../kirby-seo-telemetry/SKILL.md` Module 5 §5.10.2 (**GSC clicks + impressions on commercial service and attribute pages**, **analytics sessions flowing from attribute bridges into service pages**, and **leads/bookings per attribute page** — never aggregate top-of-funnel blog pageviews).
   - Audit brand-vs-attribute visibility across logged-out multi-run prompts (`ChatGPT`, `Perplexity`, `Google AI Overviews`) per `../../kirby-seo-telemetry/SKILL.md` Module 9 §9.6 Measurement Procedure.
