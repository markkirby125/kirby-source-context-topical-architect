# Source-Context & Topical Architect (Kirby)

[![Kirby Skills Collection](https://img.shields.io/badge/Kirby_Skills-Collection-blue?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/markkirby125/kirby-skills-collection)

Bulk keyword-export topical maps and bloated generic informational blogs risk collapsing under Google's Nov 2026 core update (`86 of 172` audited sites in Kasra Dash's Nov 10, 2026 core-update cohort — preliminary mid-rollout audit conducted Nov 14, 2026; single-source, directional, unverified — drifted away from what they actually sell). In one audited `"dentist"` export, pulling `379,000` keywords swept in `"dog dentist"`, while in one audited `"braces"` export pulling `133,000` swept in `"trousers"`, `"knee"`, and `"belt"` (single-source examples).

**The solution:** `kirby-source-context-topical-architect` enforces Week-1 Entity Identity (`Max 2 Things`), explicit **Wanted vs. Unwanted Attribute Boundaries**, **Core-First service-page coverage** before any informational publishing, **semi-automated up-to-8 eligible direct commercial competitors' sitemap `QDP/QDH/QDS`** topical mapping, and **single natural in-body contextual buyer-journey bridges** replacing redundant 20-link location blocks.

Sources: Kasra Dash (Nov 12–18, 2026) & Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR (Nov 11, 2026).
